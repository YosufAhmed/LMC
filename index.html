Engineering Heritage

Direct-Drive Focus

Linear Motor Company (LMC Precision) designs, qualifies, and manufactures high-performance electromagnetic direct-drive stages, ironless slotless coil assemblies, and rare-earth magnetic tracks for critical defense, aerospace, semiconductor, and optical scanning applications.

Built around the operational discipline of aerospace flight-hardware manufacturing, LMC eliminates mechanical transmission errors—such as backlash, ball-screw whip, and cyclic pitch hunting—delivering sub-nanometer positional repeatability with high dynamic bandwidth ($>20\,\text{G}$ peak acceleration).

Class 1,000 Cleanroom Winding
Proprietary potting and coil-encapsulation techniques eliminate outgassing for high-vacuum ($10^{-7}\,\text{Torr}$) and optical laser scanning payloads.
AS9100D Quality Assurance
Traceability from melt to motion. Full serialized raw-material certifications, magnetic flux profile mapping, and Form 8130-3 airworthiness documentation.
Advanced Halbach Track Arrays
Precision-ground magnetic tracks utilizing ultra-high-grade Neodymium NdFeB (N52H, $120^\circ\text{C}$ rated) magnets with zero cogging or parasitic torque ripple.
Drive & Controller Interoperability
Pre-tuned for standard multi-axis controllers and digital servo drives (including Aerotech A3200 / Ndrive, Elmo, Beckhoff, and Delta Tau).
